# Aetheria logo directions

These are five visual directions for the product promise: read a long study document in shorter language while keeping the original source visible. They are concept images, not production vector artwork. The current site mark stays in place while a direction is chosen.

| # | Direction | What the mark says | Palette |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Folded source](01-folded-source.png) | A document opens into a clear reading. | Deep teal `#075A57`, mint `#CFEFDF`, ivory `#F7F4EC` |
| 02 | [Clear line](02-clear-line.png) | Dense lines resolve into a shorter line. | Navy `#17364A`, aqua `#62BFB3`, ivory `#F7F4EC` |
| 03 | [Paired reading](03-paired-reading.png) | The source and its explanation stay side by side. | Aubergine `#4D3B62`, sage `#BFD8CC`, cream `#F7F1E8` |
| 04 | [Annotated excerpt](04-annotated-excerpt.png) | One important passage is called out within the document. | Pine `#153F3B`, terracotta `#B65E43`, mint `#D0F0DC` |
| 05 | [A fold](05-a-fold.png) | An A monogram is made from two unfolding pages. | Graphite teal `#243F42`, cobalt `#416B98`, mint `#CFEFDF` |

Open [the comparison board](index.html) to see all five on the site's mint background. Once one is selected, refine its geometry, test it at favicon size, and draw a clean SVG and wordmark.
